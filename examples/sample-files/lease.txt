RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. Term. Twelve months starting 1 March.
2. Rent. Due on the first of each month by bank transfer.
3. Pets. No pets are allowed without written consent of the landlord. A tenant who keeps a dog or cat with
   consent pays a pet deposit of 300 and is responsible for any damage to the property.
4. Noise. Quiet hours are 10pm to 7am.
5. Termination. Either party gives sixty days notice.
